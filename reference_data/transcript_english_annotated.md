## English Translation with Timestamp Annotations

### [00:00 - 00:15] The Hook & EM Scanning
> *"This is so terrifying that my hands were literally shaking after seeing this! Recently, Google Research conducted an experiment where they sliced a fruit fly's brain into thousands of ultra-thin slices. After slicing, they took millions of 2D images using an electron microscope, and then..."*

**Scientific Annotation**:
- **Serial-Section Transmission Electron Microscopy (ssTEM)**: Slicing biological tissue at ~40nm thickness and imaging at nanometer resolution to reveal individual lipid bilayers, synaptic vesicles, and post-synaptic densities (PSDs).
- **Scale**: Over 100 terabytes of volumetric electron microscopy imagery generated from a single adult male *Drosophila melanogaster* central nervous system (CNS).

### [00:15 - 00:30] Flood-Filling Networks & Connectome Release
> *"...to process that massive dataset into a 3D reconstruction, they used Flood-Filling Networks (FFNs) and deep learning architectures. And after achieving all this, they virtually released the complete fruit fly brain structure—with all its neurons and all its synaptic connections! And now people are making fruit flies solve Rubik's cubes..."*

**Scientific Annotation**:
- **Flood-Filling Networks (FFNs)**: A 3D convolutional neural network architecture created by Google Research (Januszewski et al.) that iteratively segments individual neurons by following neurite paths through voxel space.
- **Connectome Dimensions**: Charts **166,691 neurons** and **~125 million synaptic connections** in the adult male brain and ventral nerve cord (MaleCNS v1.0).

### [00:30 - 00:45] Sensorimotor Stimulation & Gaming
> *"...they are making them play 3D games, teaching them programming, and who knows what else! Now that Google has made this dataset publicly available, developers are utilizing the model's sensorimotor pathways to inject external data. What is happening because of this? **Technical Annotation**:
- **Sensorimotor Loop**: Biological brains operate as closed-loop dynamical systems: `Sensory Inputs (Eyes/Antennae) → Local Interneurons → Central Complex / Mushroom Body → Descending Neurons → Motor Neurons (Legs/Wings/Muscles) → Environment Feedback`.

### [00:45 - 01:00] Minecraft & StonkFly (Bitcoin Trading)
> *"...For example, as you might have seen recently, someone ran this exact fruit fly brain simulation in Minecraft [FLY-000 neural], and even had it trade Bitcoin [StonkFly]! Look, if you want to do this too, there are 3 to 4 steps: First, download the Google DeepMind / FlyWire dataset. If you visit this repository, you'll understand how to configure this model's data..."*

**Project Annotation**:
- **StonkFly Experiment**: Created by Coinbase software engineer Alex Wormuth. Candlestick charts are rendered at 320x180 resolution and projected onto virtual photoreceptor neurons. When trades earn positive returns, **15 PAM11 dopamine neurons** are activated (positive reinforcement). When trades suffer losses or trading fees, **2 PPL101 aversion neurons** fire. The motor output decodes into Buy, Sell, or Hold actions.
- **FLY-000 [neural]**: A community experiment integrating the spiking neural network into a Minecraft bot interface where vision and proximity voxels trigger neural firing that outputs movement vectors.

> *"...After that, you have to choose a physics and body simulator. If you want motor output, you must also provide sensory inputs! **Philosophical & Engineering Annotation**:
- **The Embodiment Hypothesis**: Intelligence requires physical or simulated interaction with an environment (sensory feedback loops).
- **Human Scaling**: A fruit fly has ~166,000 neurons; a human brain has ~86,000,000,000 neurons (approx. 500,000× more). However, the foundational algorithms (LIF spiking networks, synaptic plasticity, neuromodulatory gating) share direct evolutionary homology.
